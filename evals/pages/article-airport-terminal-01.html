Lisbon airport guide

By Flight Facts staff · updated 12 October 2026

Lisbon airport has two passenger buildings and a short walkway between them.

Meridian Air departures use Terminal 2. Check-in desks open three hours before departure and close 60 minutes before.

The metro station is a five-minute walk from arrivals and runs every ten minutes until the early morning.
