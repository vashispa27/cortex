Start tomato seedlings indoors in February and plant tomatoes 60 cm apart once the last frost has passed. Stake them at planting time. How do I protect the beds over winter?

ChatGPT said:

Cover bare soil with 5 cm of straw mulch so rain does not compact it. A layer of leaves works too, but straw is lighter and lets the soil breathe. Water the beds once before mulching so the moisture is locked in underneath.

When do I plant garlic?

ChatGPT said:

Sow garlic cloves on 24 October, pointed end up, 15 cm apart and 5 cm deep. Choose firm, plump bulbs from a seed supplier rather than the supermarket, because they are less likely to carry disease. Water them in once and then leave them alone until spring shoots appear.
