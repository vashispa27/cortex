A Weekday Sourdough in 6 Hours

By Dale Okafor · 2 October 2026 · 5 min read

You do not need a long cold ferment to get a good sourdough loaf. A warm, fast schedule fits around a working day.

The schedule

  • Mix flour, water and 30 percent starter at 68% hydration.
  • Bulk ferment for 6 hours at 27 °C.
  • Shape and proof on the counter for 90 minutes.

Baking

Bake in a preheated Dutch oven at 230 °C for 25 minutes lid on, then 15 minutes lid off.

Make it fit your day

Feed your starter the night before and mix the dough as soon as you get up. By the time you are back from work the dough is ready to shape, and the loaf is out of the oven before dinner.

If the kitchen is cold, put the dough in the oven with only the light on. A steady warm spot keeps the schedule predictable from one loaf to the next.

  • Use a clear container so you can watch the bubbles.
  • Mark the starting level with a rubber band.
  • Cover with a damp cloth, not a tight lid.
