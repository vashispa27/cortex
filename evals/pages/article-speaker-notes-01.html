Writing speaker notes

By Hana Ito · 3 October 2026 · 4 min read

Speaker notes are a prompt for you, not a script to read aloud. Write the point of the slide, one example and the transition.

Keep speaker notes under 120 words per slide so you can scan them in a glance while presenting.

Rehearse once with the notes hidden to check you can carry the talk without them.
