How should I time my meals around training?

ChatGPT said:

Eat a meal with about 40 g of protein and a moderate amount of carbohydrate two to three hours before lifting. Afterward, have another protein-rich meal within a couple of hours; the exact minute matters far less than hitting the daily total.

If you train early and cannot stomach a full meal, a banana and a small yogurt thirty minutes beforehand is enough. Keep caffeine to the first half of the day so it does not cut into your sleep.

  • Post-workout: eggs, rice, vegetables
  • Evening: fish or tofu with a large salad
