Rope management for beginners

By Tomas Alvarez · 6 October 2026 · 5 min read

A tidy rope keeps both partners safe. Flake the rope on a tarp before every pitch so it feeds without tangles.

Slack

When belaying a leader, leave about 1 m of slack in the rope between climber and belayer so a clip never gets pulled tight. Take in slack as soon as the climber stops moving.

A belayer who stands too far from the wall adds slack to the system and lengthens any fall.
