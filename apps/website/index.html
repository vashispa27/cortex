A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions.

A little more context about Cortex.

What is Cortex?+

Cortex is a macOS memory assistant in development. It helps you search text from your computer activity, recall details, and answer questions with links back to the evidence.

Does it record my screen?+

Cortex reads text exposed by the foreground app through macOS Accessibility. It doesn't create a video recording of your screen. Some apps and content don't expose readable text, so coverage varies.

Does everything stay on my device?+

Your activity archive and search index stay on your Mac. If you configure a cloud model, your question and relevant context are sent to that provider to generate an answer. Provider privacy and retention terms apply.

Can I choose what Cortex remembers?+

Capture starts only when you enable it. The desktop app includes start, pause, stop, and app exclusions. Private or unknown browser contexts and protected fields are skipped by the desktop capture pipeline.

Can I use it with my AI tools?+

Cortex includes an MCP bridge for compatible AI clients to access its shared memory and task workspace. Setup is documented in the repository.

Can I download it yet?+

A public installer isn't available yet. If you'd like to explore the project now, the source and macOS build instructions are available on GitHub.
